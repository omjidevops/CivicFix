How CivicFix Works

A seamless, transparent four-step journey from civic complaint to verified resolution.

01

Report

Citizen reports a civic problem.

02

Verify

Authorities verify the complaint.

03

Resolve

The responsible department works on the issue.

04

Confirm

Citizen confirms that the issue has been resolved.
